FAQ

How does the sensitivity converter work?

Each game turns your view by a fixed angle per mouse count (its “yaw”), multiplied by your sensitivity. We match the degrees turned per centimetre of mouse movement between the two games, so your muscle memory transfers 1:1.

What is cm/360?

The distance in centimetres you move your mouse to turn 360° in game. It is the only fully game-independent way to compare sensitivity.

What is eDPI?

Effective DPI = in-game sensitivity × mouse DPI. It compares players in the same game only, because yaw differs between games.

Does FOV change my sensitivity?

Hip-fire 360° distance does not change with FOV in most games, but how fast the screen appears to move does. For scoped aim, use the game’s ADS multiplier setting.
