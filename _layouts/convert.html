How the {{ page.na }} to {{ page.nb }} conversion works

{{ page.na }} turns your view by {{ page.ya }}° per mouse count at sensitivity 1; {{ page.nb }} turns {{ page.yb }}°. Dividing the two gives the multiplier {{ page.m }}. If you change DPI as well, the converter above adjusts for it, so the physical distance per 360° stays identical.

After converting

  1. Set the new value in {{ page.nb }} and turn off mouse acceleration.
  2. Play 2–3 short sessions; small adjustments of ±5% are normal because FOV makes speed feel different.
  3. Lock it in with 10 minutes of Tracking and Flick drills.
